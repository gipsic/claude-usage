# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

**Expected:** be welcoming and patient; assume good faith; give and accept
constructive review; focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Unacceptable:** harassment, insults or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct
which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues and PRs that
don't align with this code, and may ban contributors for repeated or serious
violations. Report problems to the maintainers privately via
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/gipsic/claude-usage/security/advisories/new)
(it works for conduct reports too) or by contacting a maintainer listed in
CODEOWNERS. All reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
